my-bot privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

my-bot is a private, single-user application. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads, drafts, sends and organizes email in Gmail, and reads and edits events in Google Calendar, for the Google accounts the owner connects.

How the data is used

Data from Google is used only to carry out the owner's own requests, such as summarizing the inbox, drafting a reply or adding a calendar event. Actions that send email or change or delete calendar events require the owner's explicit approval each time.

my-bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train any model of ours, and not shared with other people. To produce answers, the text of the owner's own requests and the relevant email or calendar content is processed by the AI service the owner has chosen to power the assistant.

Deleting access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored tokens from their computer.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this repository's GitHub page.
